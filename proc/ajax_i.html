public subroutine AJAX_I(nrho_r, rho_r, iflag, message, CUR_I_R, CUR_F_R, CUR_IMN_R, CUR_IMX_R, CUR_FMN_R, CUR_FMX_R)

AJAX_I gets the enclosed toroidal and external poloidal currents for a set of flux surfaces from the covariant components of B, as well as maximum and minimum values for use as a diagnostic on the accuracy of the stream function

public subroutine PARKS_DRIFT(W, r_p, ne_inf, Te_inf, R, B, M0, T0, cA_inf, beta_inf, kap_c, beta_ratio, Sigma_0, c_0_bar, Psi_int, DelR)

PARKS_DRIFT calculates the change in pellet position Delta R based on an ad-hoc scaling law. See Parks et al. (2000)

We use the same convention as Parks but also include the corresponding PELLET parameters along with their units.

------------------------------------------------------------------# Calculating scalar quantities ------------------------------------------------------------------#
